# New units, v0.2.0

Built-in image generation, using assets/nobunaga-idle.png as a style reference.

Saved assets: assets/mitsuhide-atlas-v020.webp and assets/kazutoyo-atlas-v020.webp.

Mitsuhide prompt: four equal transparent atlas tiles (idle, complete head portrait, normal sword attack, special silver crescent slash with blue petals); match established chibi manga samurai style; silver/white armor, indigo cords, helmet with blue bellflower motif; consistent identity, complete head and feet, no UI frame or text.

Kazutoyo prompt: same four transparent tiles and style; practical emerald and brown armor, simple helmet, spear, green wind special effect; friendly determined expression; modest equipment; no glitter, sparkle, UI frame or text.

Rarity assignments are a provisional game-design choice based on general recognition, not a survey or a judgement of the historical person's merit. Existing three units remain legend; Mitsuhide is rare; Kazutoyo is normal. No super-rare unit is introduced yet, but its gold visual treatment is supported.
