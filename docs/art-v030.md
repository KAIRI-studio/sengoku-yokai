# Pop redesign v0.3.0
Built-in image generation was used for all new raster illustration assets.

Saved assets:
- assets/pop-characters-v030.webp: six full-body characters, 3×2 atlas, transparent.
- assets/pop-skills-v030.webp: five skills and tengu fan, 3×2 atlas, transparent.
- assets/pop-courtyard-v030.webp: portrait temple courtyard.

Character prompts: use previous art only for identity/colors; six equal atlas cells; consistent 2-head chibi samurai; very thick smooth outlines, large rounded shapes, 5–6 flat colors, no intricate armor engraving, no hair strands, no realistic metallic shading or UI frame. Nobunaga black/gold/red cape, Yukimura red and six coins, Kenshin blue/white hood, Mitsuhide silver/indigo bellflower helmet, Kazutoyo green/brown spear, tengu red face/white beard/black wings/leaf fan. Follow-up simplified hair and armor further and removed background/shadows outside silhouettes.

Skill prompt: six transparent sticker icons with thick brown outlines and 4–5 colors: fire dragon/katana, fiery spear, ice crystals/katana, bellflower/silver sword, green spear/wind, leaf fan/wind; no complex explosions, fine textures, panels or text.

Background prompt: simple picture-book temple at upper 20–25%, round trees, puffy clouds and broad cream courtyard filling lower 75%, only a few edge grass clumps; no characters, text, realistic shadows or dense scenery.

UI was rebuilt as native HTML/CSS/SVG: flat coral, gold, silver and green rarity surfaces; rounded controls; few simple glints for legend/super/rare, none for normal. The existing saved-team key remains unchanged. Body artwork remains the same size during attacks; only movement and illustrated effects animate.
